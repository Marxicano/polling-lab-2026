How to read this page. Unofficial returns are partial and change as ballots are counted; they are never treated as final. Errors are computed only from certified results (scores) or from unofficial counts reporting 100% of ballots or precincts (labeled provisional). A recount suspends what depends on the recounted ballots: the whole race in ranked-choice states, only the winner during a Georgia runoff recount. The frozen v19 snapshot is the primary comparison; v20 is a secondary, post-freeze comparison. No result here comes from news projections.
