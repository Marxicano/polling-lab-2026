# Recording official returns (tracker 1.1.4)

Do not enter real returns until the 1.1 re-audit is complete.

1. Use only the state election authority. Its confirmed domains are in `data/official_sources.csv`; their subdomains are accepted. A separate results host (listed under `pending_result_hosts`) can be used only after you record a domain verification: the URL of the authority's own page that links to that host, who checked it, and when (`domain_rows_TEMPLATE.csv`, then `append_domain_verification.js`).
2. Enter one complete report at a time (`new_rows_TEMPLATE.csv`): every candidate the authority lists plus its write-in total, so the votes add up exactly to `total_votes`. Both named candidates must appear exactly once with their party (Nebraska: Dan Osborn, I). All rows of a report share the same status, timestamp, source URL, reporting share and unit, and the authority's own status wording.
3. Reporting share: give the number and its unit (`ballots`, `precincts` or `counties`), or leave both blank if the authority gives no share.
4. Statuses: `unofficial` until certification; then `certified`; later corrections `amended_certified`; a recount is `recount` (suspends the race) followed by `recount_certified`. Each report needs a later timestamp than the previous one for that round.
5. List candidates individually whenever the authority does. Use at most one aggregate row (`ballot_party = OTH`) for the remaining minor candidates and write-ins; party codes are D, R, I, L, G, CON or OTH. An aggregate row never wins, and if it could hold the leader the tracker records no winner.
6. Ranked choice (Alaska, Maine): enter the final round as `round = rcv_final` only when no one has a first-round majority, listing **exactly the two final continuing candidates** individually (no aggregate row); both states tabulate until two candidates remain. Georgia: enter `round = runoff` only after a certified November count without a majority, listing exactly the top two November candidates. If a ranked-choice recount changes first-choice totals, also record them as a `recount_certified` first-round report.
   - **Maine: Round 0 is not RCV Round 1.** The Secretary of State's first-choice totals (election-night style results) are what Maine's ranked-choice glossary calls **Round 0**. The later RCV tabulation report starts at **Round 1**, which can differ slightly because a ballot with a blank first choice counts for its next ranked choice. Enter **Round 0** as `round = first`; do not substitute the RCV report's Round 1. Enter the RCV report's final round (two continuing candidates) as `rcv_final`. Keep links to both documents.
   - **Maine: two denominators.** The candidate rows and `total_votes` stay the candidate-vote count (all candidates plus the write-in total); that is the registered polling-error denominator. Maine's first-round majority test uses a different denominator: all ballots cast for the office, including blank and overvoted first rankings (21-A M.R.S. §723-A(2)). On the Round 0 report, fill `ballots_cast_for_office`, `blank_first_rank`, `overvote_first_rank` (and `excluded_first_rank` if any ballot ranked an excluded candidate first) from the Secretary of State's own counts, with `denominator_source_url` pointing to the page that shows them. They must reconcile exactly: ballots cast = candidate votes + blank + overvoted + excluded. Never enter blank or overvoted ballots as candidates or as OTH.
   - **If Maine's counts are missing:** when any candidate has more than half of the candidate votes, the tracker withholds the first-round winner for human review. If the Secretary of State then publishes an official ranked-choice count, enter its final round: by law (21-A M.R.S. §723-A(2)) the count goes to rounds only when no candidate had more than half of all ballots cast, so the tabulation is the authority's own finding. A certified final round decides the winner, flagged until the Round 0 counts are entered (as an `amended_certified` first-round report with the ballots-cast fields). If the recorded counts show a majority while a final round exists, the tracker refuses them: stop and escalate.
   - **Alaska:** enter the Division of Elections' initial tabulation (each ballot counted for its highest-ranked continuing candidate) as `round = first`. Alaska's majority test is more than half of the **active** ballots (AS 15.15.350(d)); inactive ballots (blank, overvote, two consecutive skipped rankings) are excluded, so `total_votes` (active ballots) is both the polling and the legal denominator. The ballots-cast fields may be recorded for reference but do not enter Alaska's test.
   - **Georgia runoff replacement:** Georgia law (O.C.G.A. § 21-2-501) provides for replacing a runoff qualifier who withdraws, dies or is disqualified. If that happens, the tracker will refuse the official runoff because it requires the top two November candidates. Stop and escalate; it needs an evidence-documented special path.
7. Untracked contests: record each certified result with `append_untracked.js` (`untracked_rows_TEMPLATE.csv`). Until all 20 are recorded, they are shown as assumptions and chamber control is not computed.
8. Always run with `--dry-run` first. Never edit or delete rows. After each session, run `print_chain_heads.js` and commit its output separately.
9. When the gate opens (all 15 certified, or Jan 15, 2027), run `record_registered_scoring.js` once, without setting V20_SIMS. The record is never overwritten.

## Human verification checklist (every imported report)
The tracker checks structure and official hosts; it cannot check that the numbers appear on the page. Before appending, a second person confirms:
1. The `source_url` is the specific results page or document (not a homepage) on the authority's confirmed or verified host.
2. Every candidate's votes and the total match the page exactly; the authority's status wording is copied verbatim.
3. The reporting share and its unit (ballots, precincts or counties) match the page.
4. The round is right: Maine's Round 0 and Alaska's initial tabulation as `first`; the two-candidate final tabulation as `rcv_final`; Georgia's runoff only with the top two November candidates.
5. Maine: the ballots-cast, blank and overvote counts match the Secretary of State's page and reconcile to the candidate votes.
6. Write the reviewer's name in `notes` (for example, "verified by …"). A dedicated sign-off field is still an open publication gate.
